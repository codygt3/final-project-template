First movie details: In Five Nights at Freddy's, you play as a night security guard named Mike Schmidt who just started a job at a pizza-play place called Freddy Fazbear's. The main problem is that the giant animatronic robots Freddy, Bonnie, Chica and Foxy are allowed to wander around at night so their gears don't lock up. The animatronic robots will attack you because they see you as an uncostumed robot who needs to be converted into one of their spare suits which leads to an instant death. Your office space requires you to monitor their movements through security cameras while your single battery supply lasts till the end of the night. The power outage occurs when you use the doors or lights excessively which causes the building to become fully dark and Freddy to approach you. You must survive the entire five-night period which ends at 6:00 AM but the difficulty level increases because the robots become faster during each succeeding day.

Second movie details: The story begins with the introduction of "Toy" animatronics, which shows Freddy, Bonnie, and Chica through their shiny plastic appearances that create a friendly impression while their actual behavior proves to be more dangerous. The technology detects criminals, but its malfunctioning system causes violent behavior towards adults during nighttime. Mike Schmidt has returned to his mission of safeguarding his sister Abby from ghost children. The film introduces a terrifying character known as the Marionette or the Puppet who resides inside a large music box and appears to control all other robotic characters. There are 1980s flashbacks which demonstrate how William Afton the villain executed his initial deception of children. Afton remains present at the restaurant because his spirit continues to haunt the establishment despite his entrapment in the Springtrap suit during the first movie's conclusion. The film shows multiple hints from the video games which include Withered animatronics who represent the damaged past versions of the original character group that rest in the building's hidden sections. The final confrontation requires Mike to prevent the animatronics from abducting Abby until 6:00 AM arrives.
